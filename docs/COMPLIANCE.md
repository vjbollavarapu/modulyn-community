# Compliance, Regulatory, and Data Protection

## 📋 Overview

Modulyn ERP is designed with compliance and regulatory requirements in mind, ensuring that businesses can operate legally and securely across different jurisdictions. This document outlines our approach to regulatory compliance, data protection, and KYC/KYB requirements.

---

## 🔐 KYC/KYB (Know Your Customer / Know Your Business)

### **W3F Grants Program Requirements**

Modulyn is committed to completing all required W3F KYC/KYB processes:

- ✅ **KYC Completion**: Prepared to complete Web3 Foundation KYC process
- ✅ **KYB Completion**: Prepared to complete Web3 Foundation KYB process
- ✅ **CLA Signing**: Will sign the Contributor License Agreement (CLA)
- ✅ **Legal Entity**: Registered business entity for grant administration

### **Platform KYC/KYB Implementation**

#### **User Verification Levels**

1. **Level 1: Basic Verification**
   - Email verification
   - Phone number verification
   - Basic identity information

2. **Level 2: Enhanced Verification**
   - Government-issued ID verification
   - Address verification
   - Business registration documents (for businesses)

3. **Level 3: Full Verification**
   - Biometric verification (optional)
   - Financial institution verification
   - Enhanced due diligence (EDD) for high-risk users

#### **KYC/KYB Features**

- **Automated Verification**: Integration with identity verification providers
- **Document Management**: Secure storage of verification documents
- **Compliance Monitoring**: Ongoing monitoring for suspicious activities
- **Audit Trail**: Complete audit log of all verification activities
- **Privacy Protection**: Encrypted storage of sensitive information

#### **Regulatory Compliance**

- **AML (Anti-Money Laundering)**: Transaction monitoring and reporting
- **CTF (Counter-Terrorism Financing)**: Sanctions screening
- **FATF Guidelines**: Compliance with Financial Action Task Force recommendations
- **Local Regulations**: Compliance with jurisdiction-specific requirements

## 🛡️ Data Protection and Privacy

### **GDPR Compliance (European Union)**

Modulyn complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

#### **Data Subject Rights**
- ✅ **Right to Access**: Users can request access to their personal data
- ✅ **Right to Rectification**: Users can correct inaccurate data
- ✅ **Right to Erasure**: Users can request deletion of their data ("right to be forgotten")
- ✅ **Right to Restrict Processing**: Users can limit how their data is processed
- ✅ **Right to Data Portability**: Users can export their data in machine-readable format
- ✅ **Right to Object**: Users can object to certain types of processing
- ✅ **Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making**: Transparency in automated processes

#### **Data Protection Measures**
- **Encryption**: All sensitive data encrypted at rest and in transit
- **Access Controls**: Role-based access control (RBAC) for data access
- **Data Minimization**: Collect only necessary data
- **Purpose Limitation**: Use data only for stated purposes
- **Storage Limitation**: Retain data only as long as necessary
- **Privacy by Design**: Privacy considerations built into system architecture

#### **Data Processing**
- **Lawful Basis**: Clear legal basis for all data processing
- **Consent Management**: Explicit consent for data processing
- **Data Processing Agreements**: Contracts with all data processors
- **Data Protection Impact Assessments**: DPIA for high-risk processing

### **CCPA Compliance (California, USA)**

Modulyn complies with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Consumer Rights**: Right to know, delete, and opt-out of sale
- **Non-Discrimination**: No discrimination for exercising privacy rights
- **Disclosure Requirements**: Clear privacy notices and disclosures
- **Opt-Out Mechanisms**: Easy opt-out for data sales

### **Other Regional Compliance**

- **PIPEDA (Canada)**: Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act
- **LGPD (Brazil)**: Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados
- **PDPA (Singapore)**: Personal Data Protection Act
- **Privacy Act (Australia)**: Australian Privacy Principles

## 🔒 Security and Compliance

### **Security Standards**

#### **SOC 2 Type II**
- **Security**: Protection against unauthorized access
- **Availability**: System availability and performance
- **Processing Integrity**: Accurate and complete processing
- **Confidentiality**: Protection of confidential information
- **Privacy**: Protection of personal information

#### **ISO 27001**
- **Information Security Management System (ISMS)**
- **Risk Management**: Systematic approach to security risks
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regular security assessments

#### **Other Security Standards**
- **NIST Cybersecurity Framework**: Risk-based approach to cybersecurity
- **OWASP Top 10**: Protection against common web vulnerabilities
- **CIS Controls**: Center for Internet Security controls

### **Security Measures**

- **Encryption**: AES-256 encryption for data at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- **Access Controls**: Multi-factor authentication (MFA), role-based access control

- **Incident Response**: Security incident response plan and procedures
